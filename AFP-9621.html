GENERAL CONDITIONS / AFP PROTOCOL

Conditions & measurement rules

Record environmental observations where appropriate and follow the same coordinate system throughout the measurement.

Temperature18–22 °C
Same-point variation≤ 2 °C in 8 hours
Adjacent-point difference≤ 0.5 °C per metre
Relative humidity≤ 70%
Vibration≤ 0.2 G
Laser measurementsINS 75020 / MTDI 313022

General process

  • Lengths are stated in millimetres and inches.
  • Measurement instruments and methods are specified per procedure.
  • Interferometer plots should identify the test stroke and upper/lower limits; include machine designation, acquisition date, current date, operator, location and serial number.
  • The order shown does not define the execution order. Tests may be reordered to simplify setup.
  • Repeat measurements at the customer site where transport or installation may have changed an adjustment.

Special conditions

  • No drafts or heat sources in the measurement area.
  • Temperature departure from 20 °C adds uncertainty related to the effective expansion coefficient; use the typical minimum range of ±2 µm/(m·°C), ISO 230-2:2006.
  • For linear strokes longer than 30 m, straightness is measured in two or three segments at the same accuracy; the final chart combines the segments.
  • Laser Tracker temperature coefficient: 0 for X positioning/repeatability; automatic for Y and Z positioning/repeatability.
EQUIPMENT / INSTRUMENTS

Measurement equipment

Confirm instrument suitability, calibration and exact setup against the selected model's controlled procedure and figures.

Linear geometry and positioning

  • Laser Tracker with 1.5 in reflector.
  • Interferometer with straightness optics or positioning optics, as applicable.
  • Precision square, 1000 × 600 mm, dial indicator and granite surface plate for specified mechanical checks.

Rotary geometry

  • Laser Tracker, reflector and ball tooling where specified.
  • Dial indicator and the model-specific ball tool for C-axis checks.
  • Exact tooling, contact points and figure-dependent placements must be confirmed from the controlled procedure.
